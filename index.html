Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How does CardHawk scan cards during live streams like Whatnot or TikTok Shop?

CardHawk runs as an on-screen floating overlay on Android. When you're watching a stream on Whatnot, TikTok Shop, eBay Live, or YouTube, simply tap the floating bubble to instantly scan and identify the card on screen without leaving or pausing the stream.

Does CardHawk require an internet connection to identify cards?

No. CardHawk's core card identification runs entirely on your device, so it works without an internet connection.

Where does CardHawk get its Pokémon card prices?

Once a card is recognized, CardHawk provides direct one-tap links to check prices across PriceCharting, eBay, and TCGplayer.

Is CardHawk completely free?

Yes. CardHawk is free to download on Google Play with no mandatory subscriptions, account signups, or video ads cluttering your screen.
