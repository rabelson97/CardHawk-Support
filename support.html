Scanner Tips

  • Scan when the full card is visible.
  • Try a steadier, clearer moment if chat, controls, popups, or hands cover the card.
  • Some live apps let you hide controls with a gesture, such as swiping left.
  • Bright, even lighting usually helps.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

CardHawk is designed to identify trading cards from screen capture sessions on your device. CardHawk does not require an account, does not sell personal information, and does not include ads, purchases, or third-party analytics in the current app.

CardHawk uses screen capture permission only while you start an active scanner session. Captured frames are processed for card recognition. The release app does not intentionally upload screenshots or scanner frames to CardHawk servers.

CardHawk may use internet access to download card database updates, load card images, open support pages, and open third-party price or market links that you choose to visit. Those external sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

CardHawk may request overlay permission to show scanner controls on top of other apps, and notification permission to keep an active scanner session visible while it is running.
